# Motion Presence Capture Scorecard

This is the final visual acceptance instrument for the five-lane motion pass.
The calibrated implementation gates own mechanism and exact values; this
scorecard owns repeatable capture and taste decisions.

## Status language

Use exactly one status per lane:

- **Mechanism green:** implementation and calibrated gate satisfy the brief.
- **Capture ready:** the exact reviewed head is installed in a configured build.
- **Design ratified:** the complete capture set passes every hard veto and the lane-specific taste bar.
- **Returned:** a visible defect is named by clip, frame, and the smallest permitted quantity or mechanism that can repair it.
- **Blocked:** the evidence cannot be produced; name the missing capability.

Mechanism green is not design ratified. A beautiful label is earned only from
pixels produced by the exact head being approved.

## Capture contract

Every clip includes its exact branch/full commit hash, device model, OS version,
build identifier, normal/RM state, and a native uninterrupted 60fps recording.
Include 500ms of stillness before cause and 1000ms after final settle; keep the
initiating finger/touch indicator away from the subject. Preserve the untouched
source when making a 0.25x review export and record a second repetition for
randomised or coalesced lanes. A 30fps clip cannot close one-frame,
completion-alignment, or delayed-onset claims.

## Hard vetoes

Any one returns the lane:

- one-frame jump, mirror, snap-back, hard disappearance, or stale centre;
- visible pause with no cause, including a no-op animation delaying the next meaningful frame;
- second gesture silently cancelling, restarting, or queueing a story contrary to its contract;
- counter, stain, rail, or semantic state clearing before its visual obligation;
- motion competing with message, person, or gratitude content;
- RM retaining prohibited translation, scale, rotation, spring, delayed content, or hierarchy loss;
- two repetitions exposing a metronome, ping-pong loop, or unrelated phase lock.

## Shared taste score

Score each dimension 0, 1, or 2. Design ratification requires no hard veto, no
zero, and at least 9/10.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cause | Decorative or precedes cause | Legible after inspection | Cause and response read instantly |
| Continuity | Jump, reset, stop, or cut | Continuous but constructed | One uninterrupted physical thought |
| Temperament | Generic preset | Correct but anonymous | Distinct emotional role without theatre |
| Settle | Residue, wobble, premature disappearance | Technically stable | Rest returns attention to content exactly |
| Restraint + RM | Dominates or loses meaning | Acceptable hierarchy | Content remains sovereign in both registers |

The score aids comparison, not taste override. A calculated-feeling 9/10 is
returned with its perceptual defect named.

## MP-1 — narrative bee transitions

Capture one normal and one RM clip for like, share, feed arrival, and seal
arrival, plus rapid-trigger like and refresh-batch feed. Like is a bright
acknowledgement that leaves before celebration; share is a directional handoff;
feed is the quietest role and content remains primary; seal carries the most
gravity and remains without bounce, flourish, or an extra beat. Family
resemblance comes from authored continuity and restraint, never a reused
spring signature.

## MP-2 — native reveal

Capture PackageOpen and MemoryLane normal/RM, shortest and longest dwell, a live
normal → RM → normal switch, and a refused early tap followed by its accepted
successor. The date orients before the writing settles; the scale crest is felt,
not seen as bounce; the rail has no staircase/hold/backward pixel/final jump;
register switches preserve perceived opacity; successors begin cleanly at zero.

## MP-3 — nectar exchange

Capture normal fast/slow success, failure known at contact, failure during
stain, and RM success/failure. Departure gathers before travel; contact,
meniscus, stain, and ledger are one transfer; fast success completes stain;
failure reverses when known and restores count only at home; RM remains
responsive rather than yielding the gesture to network time.

## MP-4 — living flight

Capture same/opposite-facing departures, three rapid targets, a new tap during
landing light, and RM. Same-facing departure needs no ceremony; opposite-facing
uses one reorientation wheel. A second request cannot panic the bee: the current
errand lands with dignity before the latest leaves. Landing light owns its place
before its first visible frame and cleanly yields to new finger ignition.

## MP-5 — press feedback

Capture icon, text/list row, and slab CTA at normal and 0.25x, rapid
press-in/out/in, normal → RM while held, and an unresolved initial preference.
Compression is immediate and inelastic; release alone springs; the two depths
remain related; RM freezes geometry while colour and haptic preserve
acknowledgement.

## Final review protocol

1. Confirm recorded hash against branch ref before viewing.
2. Watch untouched clip at speed; record first instinct before frame inspection.
3. At 0.25x, frame-step cause, peak, handoff, and settle.
4. Watch RM pair immediately after normal and judge whether the story survived.
5. Use second repetition to expose cadence or race-dependent defects.
6. Publish a verdict with clip identifiers, veto result, five scores, and either **DESIGN RATIFIED** or exact returned frame.

Colin's eye is final. Lumen owns formal ratification; returns name observed
clip/frame, movable axis, and frozen quantities.
